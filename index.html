How these suggestions are ranked

The consistency estimate is (hits + 2) / (observations + 4), using up to 20 collected appearances. This shrinks small samples toward 50%. The last five must also meet the selected minimum. Ranking uses the lower bound of a Wilson interval to favor stronger evidence. One threshold per player/statistic is shown, with the most demanding qualifying over or under line.

These are unvalidated historical consistency estimates, not calibrated next-game win probabilities. Suggested thresholds are a fixed research catalog, not verified bookmaker offers. Checking many players and lines creates selection bias. Reported absences and uncertain injury statuses are excluded when fresh reports are available. Opponent effects, minutes, starters and current rosters remain unconfirmed; the player's last observed team is used. Missing appearances can inflate hit rates. Recent logs must be within 30 days and precede the selected upcoming game. A streak is consecutive collected observations, not a certified streak across all appearances.

Confirm that the player is active and the exact line is offered. Same-player and same-game legs can be correlated; adding more suggestions does not make a parlay more likely to win. The finder will show fewer results when the evidence does not support your requested count.
