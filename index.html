Research methods & sources

Use rolling game logs, sample sizes, role and playing-time context, and chronological testing. Streaks alone do not demonstrate a betting edge. Missing appearances are excluded, not scored as losses or zeros. Integer-line ties are shown as pushes, although platform settlement rules vary.
